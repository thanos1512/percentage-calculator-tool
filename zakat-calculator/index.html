How zakat is calculated

  1. Add up the zakatable wealth you own: cash, bank balances, gold, silver, goods held for sale, money owed to you that you expect back, and investments.
  2. Subtract debts and bills that are due now.
  3. Compare the result with the nisab. If it is equal to or more than the nisab, and has stayed so for a full lunar year, zakat is due.
  4. Zakat is 2.5% of the total, which is the same as dividing by 40.

You do not pay zakat on your home, your car, furniture, clothes or the tools you use for work.

Nisab: the threshold for zakat

BasisWeightIn grams
Silver52.5 tola612.35 g
Gold7.5 tola87.48 g

Because silver is far cheaper than gold, the silver nisab is much lower, so more people owe zakat under it. Most scholars in Pakistan advise using the silver nisab when your wealth is a mix of cash, savings and gold, because it benefits more of the poor. If all your zakatable wealth is gold and nothing else, many scholars apply the gold nisab instead. If you are unsure which applies to you, ask a scholar you trust.

For deduction from bank accounts on the first of Ramadan 2026, the government set the nisab at Rs 503,529. Banks use that fixed figure for the whole year, but for your own calculation use today's silver price.

Example

Ahmed has Rs 300,000 in the bank, Rs 50,000 cash at home and 2 tola of gold, with gold at Rs 400,000 a tola and silver at Rs 9,600 a tola. He owes Rs 30,000 on a bill due now. His zakatable wealth is Rs 300,000 + Rs 50,000 + Rs 800,000 − Rs 30,000 = Rs 1,120,000. The silver nisab is 52.5 × Rs 9,600 = Rs 504,000, so zakat is due: 2.5% of Rs 1,120,000 is Rs 28,000.

Gold and silver in tola and grams

One tola is 11.6638 grams. Jewellers in Pakistan quote prices per tola, and often per 10 grams. If your jewellery is 22 or 21 carat, use the price for that purity, since it is lower than the 24-carat price.

Questions people ask

What is the zakat nisab in Pakistan in 2026?

The nisab is the value of 52.5 tola of silver, or 7.5 tola of gold. Its rupee value changes with the market. For bank deductions in Ramadan 2026 the government fixed it at Rs 503,529.

Should I use the gold or silver nisab?

Most scholars in Pakistan advise silver when you own a mix of cash, savings and gold. If you own only gold, many apply the gold nisab. Ask a scholar if you are unsure.

Is zakat due on gold jewellery that I wear?

In the Hanafi school, followed by most people in Pakistan, zakat is due on gold and silver jewellery even if it is worn. Other schools differ on this.

How do I calculate zakat on 1 tola of gold?

Multiply 1 tola by today's price per tola to get its value, add your other zakatable wealth, and if the total is above the nisab, pay 2.5% of the total.

Do I pay zakat on my house or car?

No. Zakat is not due on the home you live in, your car, household items or tools for your work. A property or car bought to sell is business stock and is counted.

My bank already deducted zakat. Do I pay again?

The bank deduction counts towards your zakat on that account. Calculate your full zakat and subtract what the bank already took.
