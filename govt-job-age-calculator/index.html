How age is counted for government jobs

Both FPSC and PPSC count your age on the closing date of the advertisement, not the date you apply or the test date. Your date of birth is taken from your matriculation certificate or its equivalent. For competitive exams such as CSS and PMS, the commission sets a fixed cut-off date in the exam notice instead.

An upper age limit of 30 years means you can apply until you complete 31 years. Someone aged 30 years, 11 months and 20 days is still within a 30-year limit. The calculator shows the exact last closing date you remain eligible for.

PPSC adds one day when it counts age. Its own example: a candidate born on 15 August 1970 was 27 years, 5 months and 24 days old on a closing date of 7 February 1998. Tick "Count the closing date as a full day" to use this method. It only matters if you are within a day or two of a limit.

FPSC age relaxation

FPSC applies the Initial Appointment to Civil Posts (Relaxation of Upper Age Limit) Rules, 1993. You can claim one of the categories below, plus the 5-year general relaxation on top.

CategoryExtra years
General relaxation, for everyone5
Permanent residents of AJK or Gilgit-Baltistan, recognised tribes of tribal areas, Scheduled Castes and the Buddhist community3
Government servants, including contract employees, with 2 years of continuous service on the closing date10, up to a maximum age of 55
Retired or released armed forces officers and personnelYears actually served, up to 15
Widow, son or daughter of a federal civil servant who died in service5

Employees of nationalised banks, the State Bank, WAPDA and other autonomous or semi-government bodies do not count as government servants for this relaxation. You need proof of the category you claim, such as a departmental permission certificate or a domicile-based certificate.

Example. An advert for a BS-16 post sets the age limit at 18 to 25 and closes on 20 November 2026. A candidate born on 10 March 1996 is 30 years, 8 months and 10 days old that day. With the 5-year general relaxation the limit becomes 30, so they can apply. They stay eligible for adverts closing up to 9 March 2027, the day before their 31st birthday.

PPSC age relaxation

PPSC's own guidance gives a general relaxation of 5 years for men and 8 years for women for posts where the candidate meets the other requirements. Time spent in regular or contract service with the Punjab government is also left out when counting age, but the total cannot go above 35 for Combined Competitive Examination posts. Relaxation for federal or autonomous-body employees applies only if the advert says so.

Punjab sometimes grants one-off relaxations too. For example, it allowed PMS candidates up to age 32 for the 2026 and 2027 exams. Always read the advert for the post you are applying to.

Other commissions and departments

KPPSC, SPSC, BPSC, AJK PSC and departments that recruit directly (FIA, police, WAPDA and others) publish their own limits and relaxations in each advert. Choose "Other" and enter the relaxation the advert gives you.

Questions people ask

Is age counted on the closing date or the test date?

On the closing date of the advertisement for FPSC and PPSC posts. Competitive exams like CSS and PMS use a fixed cut-off date stated in the exam notice.

If the upper age limit is 30, can I apply at 30 years and 6 months?

Yes. You stay within a 30-year limit until you complete 31 years. The calculator shows the exact last closing date you can apply for.

Can I combine two FPSC age relaxations?

You can combine the 5-year general relaxation with one other category. If you qualify for two other categories, such as government service and AJK domicile, you get the larger one only.

Does the general age relaxation apply to CSS?

CSS has its own age rules in the annual CSS notice, so check that notice rather than the general recruitment rules.

Which date of birth is used?

The one on your matriculation (SSC) or O-Level certificate. If that certificate has no date of birth, commissions accept a school leaving certificate with a birth certificate or CNIC.

I'm a government employee. How much relaxation do I get from FPSC?

Up to 10 years, as long as your total age stays at or below 55 and you have 2 years of continuous government service on the closing date. The 5-year general relaxation is added on top, still within the 55 cap.
