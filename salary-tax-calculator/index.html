How the tax is worked out

Pakistan taxes salary in slabs. Only the part of your salary inside a slab is taxed at that slab's rate, so moving into a higher slab never makes your whole salary taxed at the higher rate.

  1. Multiply your monthly taxable salary by 12 to get the annual figure.
  2. Find the slab your annual salary falls in.
  3. Take the fixed amount for that slab, then add the slab's rate on the part of your salary above the slab's starting point.
  4. Divide the annual tax by 12. That is roughly what your employer deducts each month.

Example: Rs 250,000 a month. The annual salary is Rs 3,000,000, which falls in the Rs 2.2 to 3.2 million slab. The tax is Rs 116,000 plus 20% of Rs 800,000 (Rs 160,000), so Rs 276,000 a year or Rs 23,000 a month. Under last year's 23% rate the same salary paid Rs 25,000 a month.

What counts as taxable salary

Taxable salary is your basic pay plus most allowances, such as house rent, conveyance and bonuses. Some payments are partly or fully exempt, such as medical allowance up to 10% of basic pay when your employer does not provide free treatment. Your payslip or HR department can tell you the taxable figure if you are unsure. Enter that figure here for the closest estimate.

If you joined or left a job partway through the year, your employer spreads the annual tax over the months you work there, so the monthly deduction may differ from this estimate.

Questions people ask

What is the tax-free salary limit in Pakistan for 2026-27?

Salary up to Rs 600,000 a year, which is Rs 50,000 a month, is not taxed.

How much tax will I pay on a salary of Rs 100,000 a month?

Rs 500 a month. Rs 100,000 a month is Rs 1.2 million a year, and 1% of the Rs 600,000 above the tax-free limit is Rs 6,000 a year.

Did the 2026-27 budget reduce salary tax?

Yes, for salaries above Rs 2.2 million a year (about Rs 183,000 a month). The rates for those slabs went down, and the surcharge on salaries above Rs 10 million was removed. Lower salaries pay the same as in 2025-26.

Does moving into a higher slab mean all my salary is taxed at the higher rate?

No. Only the part of your salary inside each slab is taxed at that slab's rate. A raise always leaves you with more money after tax.

Why is my employer deducting a different amount?

Your employer may be treating some allowances as exempt, adjusting for tax already deducted earlier in the year, or including a bonus. Ask HR for your tax computation if the difference is large.

Do I still need to file a tax return if tax is deducted from my salary?

If your taxable income is above the limit, filing a return keeps you on FBR's Active Taxpayer List, which lowers the withholding tax you pay on things like bank transactions, property and vehicle registration.
