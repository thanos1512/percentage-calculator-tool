About HisaabPK

HisaabPK is a set of free calculators for everyday money and paperwork questions in Pakistan. Hisaab means working out the sums, and that is all this site does: it takes the rules that affect you, like tax slabs, electricity tariffs and age limits, and applies them to your numbers.

Where the figures come from

Every calculator is based on official rules: the Finance Act and FBR for income tax, NEPRA's notified tariff for electricity, FPSC and PPSC instructions for age limits, and IBCC and university rules for grades. Each page shows when its figures were last checked and links to its sources. When a budget or tariff changes, the pages are updated.

How the calculators are checked

Every calculator is tested against worked examples, including examples published by the official bodies themselves, such as PPSC's own age calculation.

A note on accuracy

The results are estimates to help you plan. For decisions that matter, such as a tax return, a job application or a large zakat payment, confirm with the official source, your employer, or a scholar. See the terms and disclaimer.
