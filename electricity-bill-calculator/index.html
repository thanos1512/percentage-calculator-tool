How your bill is calculated

Non-protected homes pay one rate on every unit: the rate of the slab their total falls in. At 250 units, all 250 units are charged at Rs 33.10.

Protected homes get the benefit of the lower slab. At 150 units, the first 100 units are charged at Rs 10.54 and the other 50 at Rs 13.01.

On top of the energy charge, the bill adds:

  • Fixed charges, multiplied by your sanctioned load in kW. These were introduced for homes in 2026.
  • Financing cost (FC) surcharge: Rs 3.23 per unit, or Rs 1.43 per unit for protected homes.
  • Fuel price adjustment (FPA): changes every month and can be a charge or a refund. Enter it if you know this month's figure.
  • Electricity duty: a provincial levy of about 1.5%. Some provinces have stopped charging it, so untick it if your bill shows none.
  • GST of 18% on the charges above.
  • Income tax of 7.5% if the bill is above Rs 25,000 and you are not on the Active Taxpayer List.

The 200-unit line

Crossing 200 units costs a protected home far more than one extra unit. At 200 units a protected home with 2 kW load pays about Rs 3,866. At 201 units the whole bill moves to the non-protected 201 to 300 slab and comes to about Rs 9,561, and the home loses protected status for the next six months. The calculator warns you when you are just above a slab line and shows how much you would save by staying below it.

Questions people ask

Are LESCO, IESCO and MEPCO rates different?

No. Household rates are set nationally by NEPRA, so LESCO, IESCO, GEPCO, FESCO, MEPCO, PESCO, HESCO, SEPCO, QESCO and TESCO all use the same per-unit rates. Monthly fuel adjustments can differ slightly, and K-Electric publishes its own adjustments.

What does protected consumer mean?

A home that used 200 units or fewer in each of the last six months. Protected homes pay much lower rates. Using more than 200 units in any month removes the status for the next six months.

Why did my bill jump after 200 units?

Above 200 units you are billed at non-protected rates, and all your units are charged at the slab rate your total falls in. One unit over 200 can more than double the bill.

What are the fixed charges on my bill?

Since 2026, household bills include a monthly fixed charge per kW of sanctioned load, from Rs 200 to Rs 675 per kW depending on your slab. Lifeline homes pay none.

Where do I find my sanctioned load?

It is printed near the top of your bill, usually as Sanctioned Load or Load (kW).

How accurate is this estimate?

It uses the notified rates and the standard taxes, so it should be close for the energy part of the bill. Items that change each month, like the fuel price adjustment, arrears and quarterly adjustments, will make your actual bill differ.
