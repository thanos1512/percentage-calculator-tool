How to calculate percentage from marks

Divide your obtained marks by the total marks and multiply by 100.

Example. 1020 marks out of 1200: 1020 ÷ 1200 = 0.85, and 0.85 × 100 = 85%. On the new IBCC scale that is grade A.

New grading system for matric and inter

The Inter Board Committee of Chairmen (IBCC) introduced a new grading scale for SSC and HSSC, phased in from 2023, and raised the pass mark from 33% to 40%. The Federal Board, Sindh boards, AJK Mirpur, Karakoram, AKU-EB and others have started using it.

Questions people ask

How do I calculate my matric percentage?

Divide your obtained marks by the total marks (usually 1200) and multiply by 100. For example, 960 out of 1200 is 80%.

What grade is 85% in the new system?

Grade A, described as excellent. 90% and above is A+, and 95% and above is A++.

What is the passing percentage in matric and inter now?

Under the new IBCC policy the pass mark is 40%. Boards still on the old system use 33%.

How many marks out of 1200 do I need for A+?

For A+ on the new scale you need 90%, which is 1080 out of 1200. On the old scale A+ starts at 80%, which is 960 out of 1200.

How do I find 20% of a number?

Multiply the number by 20 and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 5,000 is 1,000. The quick calculator on this page does it for you.
