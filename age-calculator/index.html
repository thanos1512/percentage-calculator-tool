How exact age is worked out

The calculator counts whole years since your date of birth, then whole months after your last birthday, then the days left over. When the day of the month in your birth date is later than today's, it borrows the days of the previous month, which is how ages are counted on official forms.

Example. Someone born on 15 August 2000 is 26 years, 2 months and 18 days old on 2 November 2026.

If you were born on 29 February, your birthday is taken as 28 February in years that are not leap years.

Checking your age for a job advert

Government job adverts count your age on the closing date and often allow age relaxation. Use the government job age calculator to check eligibility with FPSC or PPSC relaxation added.

Questions people ask

How do I calculate my age from my date of birth?

Subtract your birth year from the current year, then take one off if your birthday has not come yet this year. For months and days, count whole months since your last birthday and the days left over. The calculator does all of this for you.

What day of the week was I born?

Enter your date of birth and the result shows the weekday you were born on.

Can I check my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the "Age on this date" field to any date.
