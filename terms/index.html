Estimates, not advice

The calculators give estimates based on published rules and the figures you enter. They are not tax, legal, financial or religious advice. Rules change, and your own situation may include details a calculator cannot account for.

  • Tax: your actual tax depends on your full income, exemptions and FBR's rules. Confirm with your employer, FBR or a tax adviser.
  • Electricity: your actual bill includes monthly adjustments and other charges set by NEPRA and your distribution company.
  • Job eligibility: the wording of each advertisement and the decision of the recruiting commission are final.
  • Zakat: for questions about your own circumstances, consult a scholar.
  • Grades: your board's or university's result is final.
