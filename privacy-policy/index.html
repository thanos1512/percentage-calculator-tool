Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

What you type into the calculators

All calculations run in your own browser. The numbers and dates you enter are not sent to us or stored on any server. The only thing saved on your device is your light or dark mode choice, if you change it.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel may record basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and performance.

Advertising and cookies

This site may show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ads Settings, and learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The site may also use analytics to count visits. These tools may use cookies, which you can block in your browser settings.

Fonts

Text is displayed with a font loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the font is downloaded.

Children
